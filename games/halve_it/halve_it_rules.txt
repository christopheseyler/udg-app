halve-it

1 to 8 players
No player reorder screen 

Options

game mode

	 9 Rounds Way (15 to Bulleye) : 15, 16, 17, Any Double, 18, 19, 20, Any Triple, Bull eye
	 Extendend Way (12 to Bulleye) : 12, 13, 14 , Any Double, 15, 16, 17, Any Triple, 18, 19, 20, Bull eye
	

Starting Score : initial score you get when the game begins
	0 (default)
	40
	80
	

Rules

Each round is associated to a specific target (according to the game mode). Each player throws his 3 darts attempting to hit the target.
If the target is hit, the player increases his score by the value x multiplier
If the player fails to hit the target with his three darts, his score is halved (divided by 2, rounded down)

Winning

At the end of the last round, the player with the higher score wins
